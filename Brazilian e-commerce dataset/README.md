# Shipping Delay Analysis – Brazilian B2B E-Commerce

## 🧭 Context
This project investigates shipping delays in a Brazilian B2B e-commerce dataset (Olist). It explores delivery performance, identifies delay drivers, and suggests operational improvements aimed at enhancing customer satisfaction and logistics efficiency.

## 📌 Problem Statement
Assess delivery performance and uncover patterns in shipping delays. Ultimately, enable data-driven strategies to reduce delays, optimize routing/carrier selection, and improve customer loyalty.

## 📊 Key Findings
- X% of shipments are delayed; regions A, B, C show statistically higher delay rates.
- Delay probability increases by Y% when shipping heavy/large items.
- Carrier C outperforms competitors with on-time delivery rate Z%.
- Seasonal spikes in delays observed during November–December (holidays).
  
## 🛠 Metrics Computed
- On-Time Delivery Rate (monthly and by segment)
- Delivery Times per Product Categories
- Processing time per stages through the order-to-delivery process.
- Delay rates by states and cities

## 💡 Additional Analyses to be added later on.

- Time series seasonality analysis.
- Cohort analysis on repeat customers' experience.
